# Module 05 – Exercises

Use the `employees` table created by `practice/01-filtering-data-practice.sql`.

**Instructions:** Write each query yourself before checking the solutions. Select only the columns requested.

## Beginner

1. Display all employees who work in the IT department.
2. Display the names and salaries of employees earning more than 55000.
3. Find employees who are exactly 30 years old.
4. Display employees who are not in the HR department.
5. Find employees whose age is less than or equal to 28.

## Intermediate

6. Find employees who work in IT and earn more than 50000.
7. Find employees who live in Mumbai or Delhi.
8. Find employees whose salary is between 45000 and 65000, inclusive.
9. Find employees who work in IT, HR, or Finance using `IN`.
10. Find employees who are not in IT or Sales.
11. Find employees whose names start with `A`.
12. Find employees whose names contain `ar`.
13. Find employees whose names have exactly four characters.

## Challenge

14. Find IT or Finance employees who earn at least 55000.
15. Find employees in Mumbai or Pune whose age is between 25 and 32.
16. Find employees whose names end with `a` and whose salary is greater than 45000.
17. Find employees who are not in HR or Sales and earn between 50000 and 75000.
18. Find employees in IT or HR who live in Delhi or Pune and are younger than 31.
